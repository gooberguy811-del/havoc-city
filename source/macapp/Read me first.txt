HAVOC CITY for Mac

1. Drag "Havoc City" into your Applications folder, then double-click it.

2. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the app. That's because it isn't
   registered with Apple (that costs $99 a year), not because anything is wrong.
   Click "Done", then open  System Settings > Privacy & Security,  scroll down
   and click "Open Anyway" next to Havoc City. Enter your password.
   From then on it opens normally.

   Older macOS (Sonoma or earlier): right-click the app > Open > Open also works.

3. If macOS instead says the app "is damaged and can't be opened", open the
   Terminal app, paste this line and press Return, then open the game again:

   xattr -cr "/Applications/Havoc City.app"

The game updates itself: every time you open it, it loads the newest version from
gooberguy811-del.github.io/havoc-city (and keeps a copy for playing offline).

Online play uses the "Play online" code system (same as the HTML file version).
Your progress and settings are saved on this Mac.
